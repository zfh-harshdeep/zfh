THE 3 PILLARS OF ZERO-FUNDED HUSTLING

Before the strategy, you need the foundation. These are the core tenets that separate the 9-5 employee from the entrepreneur.

Pillar 1: The 'Zero-Risk' Mindset

Most people only see risk. We see zero-cost opportunity. Focus on leveraging existing, free tools and services to validate demand *before* investing a rupee. This shifts the failure penalty from financial to educational.

Pillar 2: System Over Income

Your job pays you for hours. Your hustle must pay you for systems. Build automated income streams that run while you sleep or work your 9-5. This is the only way to scale past a few thousand rupees.

Pillar 3: The Profit Layering Model

Never rely on one income stream. The key is layering small, zero-cost hustles (like service arbitrage and digital products) so your combined income creates the financial cushion you need to quit.
